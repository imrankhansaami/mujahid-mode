Make it yours

Editable by design

Every sheet is plain HTML and CSS. Change the brand, the wording, the colours, or the fields. The source is yours to adapt for a circle, a client, or your own edition.

01

Rebrand

Edit brand.js once — name, tagline, footer. It updates every sheet.

02

Restyle

Change the CSS variables at the top of print.css: accent, ink, paper.

03

Reprint

Open any sheet and print at 100% on A4. Or regenerate the PDFs from the source.

Printing: use A4, 100% scale (not “fit to page”), and enable “Background graphics”. The habit tracker is landscape; the rest are portrait. The design is deliberately ink-light so it remains readable in black and white.
Selling an edition? The files are MIT licensed, so you may adapt and sell your own version. Rebrand it, replace the content you do not own, credit the inspiration, and have every religious reference verified. Do not imply endorsement by the original programme.
