The Private Log

You do not defeat an urge at the moment of temptation. You defeat it at the door — the gaze, the device, the hour, and the room. Log the pattern, not the shame.

The emergency protocol

Rehearse it before you need it
1
HALT
Hungry, angry, lonely, or tired? Address the real need first.
2
Change the body
Stand, leave the room, wudu with cold water, drink, move.
3
Change the input
Close the screen, call your witness, go to the masjid or outside.
4
Name the truth
This costs more than it gives. Make duʿāʾ in the moment.
5
Log it
Held or slipped, write it down. The log is a map, not a courtroom.

After a slip · the return

Return today, not Monday
Stop the episode. Do not “finish” it.
Admit it to Allah without excuses; ask forgiveness.
Tell one trustworthy person.
