# The Mujāhid Mode Worksheet Pack

Ten fillable worksheets for the 60-day programme. Use them digitally or print them. Markdown is
deliberately plain so you can edit, rebrand, translate, or sell your own edition.

**How to use:** complete the worksheet in one sitting. Write in the present tense. Be specific
enough that a stranger could verify your answer.

---
