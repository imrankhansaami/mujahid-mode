# Module 08 · Digital Purity

*Tech as a tool, not a trap*

> **Thesis.** Attention is the raw material of worship and work. Whatever owns it owns your life.

**Lessons:** 1 · **Reading time:** 7 minutes · **Drills:** 2

## Lesson 23 · The Tool and the Trap

*Rebuild your relationship with the phone*

**The big idea.** You do not need to hate technology. You need to make it single-purpose, outside your bedroom, and boring by default.

The phone is the most successful attention extraction machine ever built. It is also a tool that can carry the Qur’an, your work, your family, and a thousand useful things. The difference is architecture.

> “And do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge. Indeed, the hearing, the sight, and the heart — all of those will be questioned.”
>
> — *Qur’an 17:36*

### The attention audit

For one day, write down: screen hours, pickups, the first thing you touch in the morning, the last thing at night, and the three apps that take the most. No judgment. Just the numbers.

### The architecture

- The bedroom is a screen-free zone. Phone charges outside.
- No phone for the first 30 minutes after waking and the last 60 before sleep.
- Home screen: phone, message, maps, camera. Everything else is buried.
- Notifications off by default. You check the app; the app does not check you.
- One digital sabbath window each week — a half-day, not necessarily a full day.
- If you can, keep a separate device for work and one for the rest.

### Replace, do not just remove

If you remove the scroll and leave a void, the scroll returns. Fill the space deliberately: a book by the bed, a walk after Maghrib, a conversation at the table, a hobby that uses your hands.

The goal is not a monastic life. It is a life where the tool is used and the person is not.

> “The smart one is the one who takes account of himself and works for what comes after death, and the weak one is the one who follows his desires and hopes in Allah.”
>
> — *Tirmidhī, graded ḥasan by some scholars*

### Drills

- **Run the attention audit** — *once · 10 min*
  Record screen hours, pickups, first and last use, and the top three apps today. Write the numbers in the Dopamine Reset tool.
- **Rebuild the phone** — *once · 12 min*
  Clear the home screen to four apps, turn off all non-essential notifications, and set a nightly phone curfew. Do it now, not later.

### Reflection

1. What is the phone actually giving me in the moment I reach for it?
2. What would I do with the first 30 minutes of the day if it were mine?
